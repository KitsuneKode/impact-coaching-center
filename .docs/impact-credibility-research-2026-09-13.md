# Impact Career Tutorials: public credibility and conversion research

Research date: 13 September 2026  
Scope: public business listings, first-party material, official institutional notices, local media, and publicly indexed social material.  
Constraint: this is evidence research only; no application source code was changed.

## Executive judgment

The site has enough public evidence to sell a credible local coaching centre, but it should sell the experience and the verifiable signals rather than promise exam outcomes. The best publishable proof is:

1. a real North Lakhimpur presence with matching phone numbers and email on the institute's current first-party CA-magazine site;
2. a strong local third-party listing signal on Justdial;
3. recurring public educational activity: free workshops, current-affairs material, and the ASSIST scholarship test;
4. an official college notice board that names Impact Career Tutorials in a result notice and a scholarship-test notice.

The site should not currently publish a headline such as “our students secured X posts” or imply that the institute alone produced named APSC selections. The APSC result PDF can verify that candidates appeared on the official list, but it does not establish the coaching relationship. Those stories need candidate consent and an institute-owned or candidate-owned corroborating source before publication.

## Success-story decision

There is not yet enough independent evidence for a standalone success-stories page populated with named APSC outcomes. Build the page only after the institute supplies a small evidence packet for each story:

- student's full name and permission to publish;
- exam, year, post, and result serial or official result link;
- the candidate's relationship to ICT and the relevant batch;
- a portrait or short video with permission;
- a first-person quote that the student approves;
- a corroborating official result document or candidate announcement.

Until then, a safer page is “Student outcomes and proof” with three kinds of content clearly labelled:

- **Verified public result:** links to the official exam result, with no claim that ICT alone caused the result;
- **Student story:** only with named consent and candidate confirmation;
- **Learning proof:** real lectures, faculty, free workshops, mock tests, library/study space, and the Yodha magazine.

The current codebase's two named APSC selections should remain in a verification queue. The official result source can support the result itself, but the phrase “prepared at ICT” requires an additional source or owner confirmation.

## Contradictions and claims to resolve with the owner

1. **Address:** D C Bunglow Road/Ward 8 versus NT Road/DC Bungalow versus Girls School Chariali.
2. **ADRE duration:** the first-party CA site says 12 months, while the current catalogue copy describes VICTOR 8.0 as 200+ hours. These may both be true, but the course page should explain whether one is the access period and the other is class hours.
3. **Scholarship amount:** ₹5 lakh is promotional copy from a public repost. Obtain the official ASSIST rules and clarify whether this is a total pool, maximum award, or fee-discount value.
4. **College relationship:** an official college notice board naming ICT is strong evidence of activity, but not proof of a partnership. Obtain the original notices and any permission before using “in association with”, “partner”, or “collaboration”.
5. **Current batch dates and offers:** dated campaign posts become stale quickly. Display start dates and discounts only when the owner confirms they are live.
6. **Course and app availability:** the public-facing materials describe an app and hybrid delivery. Confirm that the app, login, recordings, and checkout are currently operational before promising same-day recordings or online access.

## Recommended client-facing additions, in priority order

### 1. Improve the course discovery path

Add a small search/filter control to the Courses page only if the catalogue grows beyond the current small set. For the current three-track catalogue, a prominent segmented filter (`APSC`, `ADRE & Assam Police`, `Banking/SSC/Railway`) is clearer and faster than a search box. Add search when there are enough course pages or when the owner confirms more courses are coming.

### 2. Add a proof-led “Why ICT” block

Use the verified phone consistency, Justdial review signal, free workshops, Yodha magazine, faculty lecture links, and confirmed batch/course information. Each proof item should link to its source or say “confirmed by ICT” rather than presenting a vague trust badge.

### 3. Add a success-stories page only after evidence collection

The page is commercially valuable, but empty or weakly sourced testimonials reduce trust. Ship a lightweight page with two or three properly documented stories rather than a gallery of unsupported claims.

### 4. Add a current activity section

A small “Recently at ICT” section can show ASSIST, Yodha, free workshops, and dated batch announcements. Give every item a date and expiry policy so old offers do not remain on the homepage.

### 5. Make location conversion-safe

Use one verified map pin, a “Call/WhatsApp for directions” action, and the owner-approved landmark. This is more valuable than adding more animation or a global navigation loader.

### 6. Ask for a small owner evidence pack

Request: current Google Business Profile link and screenshots/export of rating count; exact map pin; current course catalogue and fee sheet; ASSIST rules; OPD notice PDFs; student permissions and result documents; current app/store links; and approved faculty bios. This is the shortest path to turning the site from a good presentation into a defensible sales asset.

## Sources not suitable as proof

- Search snippets and directory-generated descriptions that claim facilities, policies, or “state-of-the-art technology” without a first-party source.
- The Sentinel Assam article about Lakhimpur APSC qualifiers, because it does not mention ICT.
- Public promotional reposts as independent validation. They prove that a promotion was publicly circulated, not that the underlying offer or result was independently verified.
- Unnamed app reviews as named testimonials. They may be shown as “Google Play review” only if the current listing and text are confirmed by the owner.

## Research limitations

Instagram, Facebook, and the live YouTube channel were not reliably machine-readable in this environment. Their existence and links are present in the repository's first-party data, but individual post-level claims should be checked manually in the owner's logged-in accounts before publication. The official APSC PDF and the two OPD PDFs were also not retrievable during this run; they remain the required next verification step for success stories and college-linked claims.
