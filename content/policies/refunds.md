## Before online ordering opens

Impact Career Tutorials does not currently take online orders or payments through this website, so there is nothing to cancel or refund here yet. This is the policy that will apply once it does.

Course fees are paid through ICT's Classplus storefront or app, and their cancellation and refund terms are shown there.

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel a book order {{proposed: until it has been packed}}. Message +91 86380 61175 on WhatsApp or write to impactcareertutorials@gmail.com with your order reference. Once an order has been packed or handed to the courier it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned as described below.

If ICT cannot supply a book you have paid for, ICT cancels that item and refunds it in full.

## Damaged, wrong or missing books

If a book arrives damaged, misprinted, or is not the book you ordered, tell us {{proposed: within 7 days of delivery}}, with the order reference and a photo. ICT will {{proposed: send a replacement at no charge, or refund the book if a replacement is not available}}. You do not pay to send back a book that was our mistake.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the method you paid with. ICT starts the refund {{proposed: within 2 working days}} of agreeing to it. Your bank or payment app then usually takes {{proposed: 5 to 7 working days}} to show it.

A refund covers the book price and, when the whole order is refunded because of our mistake, the delivery charge.

## Orders collected from the centre

The same rules apply. Please check books when you collect them; it is quicker to sort out a problem at the counter.
