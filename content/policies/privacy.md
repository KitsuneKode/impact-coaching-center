## What we collect

**When you send an enquiry.** Your name, mobile number, the exam you are asking about and your preferred time of day. Your network address is used only to limit automated submissions and is not stored with your enquiry.

**If you sign in.** We verify that the mobile number is yours by sending a one-time code on WhatsApp. There is no password. We keep the number, the fact that it is verified, the date, and the name you give us. You do not need to sign in to send an enquiry.

**If you answer the three short questions after signing in.** The exam you are preparing for, whether this is your first attempt, and the batch you prefer. Every question can be skipped, and you can change or clear your answers on your account page.

**If you order books** (when online ordering opens). The books, quantities and prices, your delivery address or your choice to collect from the centre, and the order's payment and delivery status. Payments are handled by {{fill: payment provider, expected to be Razorpay}}. We receive a payment reference and whether the payment succeeded. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login.

**What staff add.** When ICT staff follow up an enquiry they may record the status of the conversation, a note of the call and a date to call back. These notes are kept with the enquiry and are visible only to ICT staff.

**Spreadsheet downloads.** The owner of ICT can download enquiries they select as a spreadsheet, to work from offline. This happens only when they choose it, never automatically, and contains only the enquiries they ticked. Staff notes are not included.

## Why we collect it

- To reply to your enquiry and help you choose a course.
- To let you sign in and keep your details, answers and orders in one place.
- To deliver books you order and deal with problems with an order.
- To understand which courses people ask about, so ICT can plan batches.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Your rights

You can:

- **See** what we hold about you, and **correct** it. Your name and answers are on your account page.
- **Delete** your account from your account page at any time. This erases your name, answers and sign-in and removes your mobile number from ICT's enquiry records. Orders are kept without your personal details, as explained above.
- **Withdraw consent** for anything that depends on it. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.
- **Complain** to our grievance officer (below), and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

For anything you cannot do on your account page, write to impactcareertutorials@gmail.com or message +91 86380 61175 on WhatsApp. We may ask you to confirm the request from the mobile number we hold, so we do not give your data to someone else.
