# English and Assamese

Outcome: every public page reads in English at today's URL and in Assamese
at `/as/...`. A first-visit dialog asks which language, the choice is kept in
a cookie, and returning Assamese readers land on `/as`. Assamese copy is drafted
here and verified by ICT before launch; anything not yet translated falls back
to English. One type system serves both scripts.

## Decisions

- **Routing.** next-intl, `localePrefix: "as-needed"`: English unprefixed, so
  no existing URL, slug or anchor changes. Pages stay statically rendered
  (`generateStaticParams` over locales plus `setRequestLocale`).
- **Preference.** Cookie `ict-lang`, one year. A cookie, not localStorage,
  because the server has to know it to send `/` to `/as` before any page
  renders. The dialog writes it; the header switch rewrites it.
- **Scope of `/as`.** Marketing pages move under `app/[locale]/`. Admin, API,
  login, register, checkout and account stay English for now; the last four
  move into an `app/(plain)` route group (URLs unchanged) that supplies the
  English chrome. `/redesign` is untouched.
- **Messages.** `messages/en.json` and `messages/as.json`, namespaced by
  component. Assamese is merged over English at load, so a missing key shows
  English, never a raw key. `scripts/i18n-review.ts` writes a side-by-side sheet
  for ICT to verify. Facts (prices, names, serials, phone numbers) stay in
  `lib/` and are not translated.
- **Digits.** Latin digits in both languages (`as-IN-u-nu-latn`) so prices and
  phone numbers read exactly as on ICT's posters. ICT can change this.
- **Type.** The Anek superfamily: Anek Latin and Anek Bangla share one design,
  so English and Assamese look like one voice. Two weights only, 700 and 400.
  Section openers use the lead pattern: a 700 sentence running into 400 muted
  text at the same size. Assamese gets looser leading (conjuncts and matras
  need the room) and no negative tracking.

## Built on top

- **Centres.** `lib/brand.ts` `centres`: North Lakhimpur (main, mapped) and
  Dhemaji (`at: null` until ICT sends the address and pin). /contact#centres
  finds the nearest from the visitor's location, with directions and a map.
- **Exam guides.** /exams and /exams/{apsc,adre,assam-police-si,banking}, both
  languages in `lib/exams.ts`, every figure from the conducting body's own
  notice or PDF, linked at the foot of each page.
- **Exam calendar.** Convex `examDates` (owner edits at /admin/calendar, draft
  then publish, audit rows `date_save`/`date_publish`). Every date needs an
  official source link. /exams/calendar groups upcoming dates by month with a
  countdown and shows recent ones below; each guide lists its next four.
  Pages stay static and re-read the calendar hourly (`unstable_cache`), and a
  failed read shows the empty state rather than breaking the page.
- **ASSIST.** /assist: what ICT has published about the scholarship test and a
  WhatsApp "tell me when it is announced". Details are on the toConfirm list.
- **Blog language.** Posts carry `language` ("en" | "as", absent reads "en").
  The editor picks it; both /blog and /as/blog list every post, their own
  language first, and a post's text carries its `lang`.
