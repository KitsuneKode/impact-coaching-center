# Promotions: a guide for ICT staff

Admin → **Promotions**. Owner, or staff given the _promotions_ permission (Admin → People).

## The four kinds

| Kind             | Where it appears                                                                           | Good for                                |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Announcement bar | The dark strip above the menu, on every page it targets                                    | One line: "Victor 9.0 is open"          |
| Top banner       | A blue band near the end of the page                                                       | A batch or free workshop, with an image |
| Pop-up           | A card in the middle of the screen, once per visit, after the visitor has read for a while | One strong offer at a time              |
| Video reel       | A row of YouTube Shorts near the end of the page                                           | Toppers, teachers, class clips          |

## Make one

1. **New promotion**, choose the kind.
2. Write the **headline** (short), **message** and **button text**. The preview on the right is what visitors see.
3. **Button link**: a page on the site (`/courses/lakhya-2027`) or a full `https://` link.
4. **Assamese wording** (optional): shown on the Assamese site. Leave it empty and Assamese visitors see the English.
5. **Where it shows**: tick sections, or tick nothing for every page. A section covers every page inside it.
6. **Starts / Ends** (optional, India time): it switches itself on and off.
7. **Save**. It stays a draft until you press **Go live**.

## Run it

- **Pause** stops it at once on every open page. Nothing is ever deleted.
- **Priority**: when two of the same kind could show, the higher number wins.
- **Hidden after closing**: days before a visitor who closed it sees it again. Editing a promotion shows it to everyone again.
- Promotions never show on checkout, sign-in or account pages.

## Is it working?

The list shows the last 30 days for each promotion: **seen**, **clicks** with the click rate, and **closed**.
A rough guide: a bar or banner clicked by 1 to 3 percent of the people who see it is normal; a pop-up 3 to 8 percent.
Lots of closes and few clicks means the offer or the wording needs changing.
