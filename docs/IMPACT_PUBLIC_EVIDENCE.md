## What is currently safe to sell

- A local, hybrid coaching institute with APSC, ADRE, Banking/SSC/Railway offerings.
- Public app-backed learning with live classes, recorded access, notes, tests, assignments, doubt support and parent-teacher communication as listed by the developer.
- Free current-affairs content through YODHA, subject to confirming the lead form and latest issue.
- A documented 2023 career-guidance event with the Lakhimpur District Commissioner.
- A public scholarship campaign and dated course promotions, presented as campaigns rather than outcomes.
- Specific named faculty and lecture links only where the owner confirms the names and the linked video still matches the teacher.

## What should not be published yet

- A generic “success stories” page populated from unverified claims.
- “X students selected”, ranks, government posts, pass rates, or “proven record” language without the candidate’s name, exam cycle, official result source and permission to publish.
- “Scholarships worth ₹5 lakh awarded” based only on the ASSIST promotional post.
- Current discounts, batch dates, fees or app availability without a fresh owner check.
- Testimonials copied from directories or the Play Store without clearly labelling them as reviews and retaining the original source link.

## Owner evidence request

Ask for a small approval pack: 3–6 student stories with written consent, exam/year/post, official result link or document, a portrait or video permission, the course/batch attended, and one sentence about the student’s experience. Also request the latest course catalogue export, current fees and batch dates, the latest YODHA issue, and an export or screen recording of the strongest Instagram/Facebook/YouTube posts.

This keeps a future success-stories page truthful, useful and easy to maintain.
