# How to show the designs

For you, not the client. A call or a message, about ten minutes.

## What you are asking for

One decision: which look the real ICT website gets. You are not asking them to
approve a finished site. You are asking for two favourites and one line on why
for each. Everything after that is built on their pick.

## Say this first

"These are 28 ways the same website could look. The courses, batches, fees,
teachers, books and free notes are the same in every one. Only the look and
feel changes. Open them on your phone first, because that is where most of your
students will see it. Pick the two you like most."

## What is real and what is a sample

Tell them up front, so nobody asks about it halfway through.

| Real, from ICT                         | Sample, until ICT adds its own                                                              |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Batches, timings, fees, teachers       | Book titles and covers                                                                      |
| Results and the officers' list         | Free-notes folders and files                                                                |
| YouTube videos and the student Shorts  | The offer code and its end date                                                             |
| Address, phone numbers, WhatsApp       |                                                                                             |
| The ASSIST name and ICT's videos on it | ASSIST date, fee, format and amounts. ICT has not given them yet, and the page says so      |
|                                        | The pop-up has no poster. The workshop poster carries past dates, so ICT adds a current one |

The forms check the details but send nothing. Say so if they try one.

## The order to show them in

Do not walk through all 28. Show the shortlist, and let them find the rest.

1. **Open the index** and read the first line out loud. Point at the heart
   button and the left and right arrow keys.
2. **Show four that look nothing alike**, in this order, a minute each:
   - **Laureate**: ICT's gold on navy. The safe, serious one.
   - **Pulse**: bold blue, young and loud.
   - **Gamusa**: red and cream, Assam first.
   - **Apogee**: black, one huge headline. The brave one.
3. **Show the same four things in each**, so they compare like with like:
   - The announcement bar at the top of the home page.
   - The offers pop-up. It opens by itself about six seconds after the home
     page loads, once per design. If you missed it or want it again, tap
     **Offers** in the bottom bar and it comes back.
   - The video in the corner. It plays on its own, muted. Tap it for sound.
   - A book page, and the free-notes folders.
4. **Show it on a phone** for the one they react to most. Open the menu.
5. **Send the link** and ask them to look at the other 24 on their own time.

If they ask "which would you pick?", answer in one sentence and say why. Then
ask which of the four felt most like ICT.

## When they say

- **"I like the colours of one and the layout of another."** That is normal and
  it works. Take the two names down. We can build one design with the other's
  colours or one section from it.
- **"Can the real photos go in?"** Yes. Several already use ICT's own photos and
  thumbnails. The real site uses all of them.
- **"Is it live?"** No. It is a preview. It sends nothing, stores nothing, and
  search engines are told to ignore it.
- **"Too many."** Send the shortlist of nine only: Apogee, Laureate, Charter,
  Vesper, Pulse, Gamusa, Notebook, Atlas, Akhor.
- **"I can't decide."** Hand them `pick-a-design.md`. Five questions, each one
  points at a few designs.

## Closing

Ask for this, in this form:

> Your top two, and one line on why for each.

The index has a "Copy my picks" button that writes this for them. They paste it
into WhatsApp or email.

Then say what happens next: we rebuild the chosen design on the real site with
ICT's own books, notes and batches, and show it again before anything goes live.
Do not promise a date here. Name one when the scope is clear.

## After they pick

- Write down the two picks and the reasons in the project notes.
- If they named something from a design they did not pick, record it too.
- Next piece of work: plan the real build from the winner. The preview stays up
  as the reference until the real site replaces it.
