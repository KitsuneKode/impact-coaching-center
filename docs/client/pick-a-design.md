# Pick a design for the ICT website

There are 28 designs. You do not need to look at all of them. Answer these five
questions and start with the ones listed. Then open the rest if you want.

Every design has the same courses, batches, fees, teachers, books and free
notes. Only the look and feel changes.

On a computer, the left and right arrow keys move between designs. Tap the
heart on the ones you like. Choose up to three.

## 1. Which language should lead?

- **Assamese first.** Sandhya, Gamusa and Akhor. Akhor shows Assamese and
  English side by side.
- **English first.** All the others.

## 2. Light or dark?

- **Light page, easy to read in daylight.** Timetable, Answer sheet, Tiles,
  Path, Mist, Season, Notebook, Atlas, Akhor, Diorama, Alloy, Rollcall,
  Charter, Meridian, Livery and Gamusa. Pulse is light too, with bold blue
  panels.
- **Dark page, feels like a studio.** Apogee, Vesper, Stage, Channel, Lamp,
  Sandhya, Sovereign, Calibre, Udoy and Clearcoat.
- **Either.** Laureate has both, and the visitor can switch.

## 3. What should a visitor see first?

- **The time of the batch.** Timetable, Tiles, Notebook, Udoy, Pulse.
- **The teachers.** Stage, Vesper, Charter.
- **The results and the officers.** Sovereign, Laureate.
- **The route to an exam, step by step.** Atlas and Path.
- **The next exam date.** Season.
- **A class on video before paying.** Channel, Mist.
- **Free books and notes.** Mist, Season, Alloy.

## 4. How should ICT feel?

- **Serious and trusted.** Laureate, Sovereign, Meridian, Charter.
- **Young and energetic.** Pulse, Rollcall, Livery, Clearcoat.
- **Proudly Assamese.** Gamusa, Akhor, Sandhya.
- **Different from every other coaching site.** Apogee, Vesper, Calibre, Diorama.
- **Plain and clear, like a good textbook.** Answer sheet, Notebook, Timetable.

## 5. Which one made you stop?

Forget the answers above. Open six designs and notice which you went back to.
That one is usually the right one.

## Send us your picks

On the first page, tap the heart on up to three designs. Put them in order,
best first, and write a few words on why for each. Then tap **Copy my picks**
and paste it into WhatsApp or email.

It looks like this:

> My picks for the ICT website:
>
> 1. Laureate: serious, looks like us (link to the design)
> 2. Pulse: the blue is ICT's (link to the design)
> 3. Gamusa: very Assamese (link to the design)

You can also take the colours of one design and the layout of another. Tell us
which, and we will build that.
