# What we need from ICT for the website

One Google Drive folder, called **ICT website**, shared with us as an
editor. Inside it, the numbered folders below, in this order: the first
ones matter most for the launch on 31 October.

Three rules for every file:

1. **Send the original.** Upload straight from the phone or camera to
   Drive. Do not send photos or videos through WhatsApp first: WhatsApp
   shrinks them and they look blurred on a laptop.
2. **Name the file by what it shows,** in English, with hyphens:
   `classroom-1-morning-batch.jpg`, `teacher-raheef-aowal.jpg`,
   `review-priya-chario-deuri-adre.mp4`.
3. **Ask before you photograph or film a student,** and keep their written
   yes (folder 05 has a one-line consent form).

## 02 Centre photos

What a parent wants to see before sending their child: the building, the
rooms, the people working in them.

| Photo                                         | How many   |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Outside, with the signboard readable          | 1–2        |
| The entrance and stairs                       | 1          |
| Each classroom, from the back, during a class | 1 per room |
| The digital classroom                         | 1–2        |
| The mock-test hall during a test              | 1–2        |
| The library or reading room, if there is one  | 1–2        |
| The front desk                                | 1          |

- **Size:** upright (portrait) 1080 × 1920 or larger, or sideways
  (landscape) 1600 × 1200 or larger. Any recent phone camera is enough.
- **Light:** daytime, lights on, windows open. No filters.
- **Dhemaji:** the same list once it opens, in the Dhemaji folder, with its
  full address and a Google Maps pin.

Today the site uses six frames cut from YouTube videos (405 × 720). They
look soft on a laptop; these photos replace them.

## 03 Centre videos

Short walk-throughs for the tour page (/campus).

- 30–90 seconds each, filmed upright (9:16), phone held steady.
- One walk from the gate to a classroom; one of a class in progress; one
  of a mock test.
- A YouTube or Instagram link is fine if it is already posted.

## 06 Student reviews (video)

The videos that convince the next student. What makes a good one:

- **Upright (9:16), 30–60 seconds,** filmed in a quiet room.
- **Name and batch said or shown** in the first five seconds.
- **One specific thing** that helped: a teacher, the mock tests, the
  notes, the library. Not "ICT is the best".
- **Subtitles** if spoken in Assamese, so English readers follow too
  (and the other way round).
- Put English-language reviews in `English/`, Assamese in `Assamese/`.
  We show each on the matching version of the site.

Already on YouTube? Paste the links into the facts sheet instead.
