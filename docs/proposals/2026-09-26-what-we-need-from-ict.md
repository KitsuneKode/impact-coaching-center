## 2. The policy pages (legally needed before taking payments)

Privacy, Terms, Refunds and Shipping are written in full. Two kinds of
highlight mark what we need from you:

- **Proposed**: a value we suggested from common practice, e.g. "within 7
  days of delivery". Say yes, or give your own.
- **ICT to fill**: a detail only you know.

The things only you can fill in:

- [ ] Legal form of the business (proprietorship, partnership or company)
      and GSTIN if registered.
- [ ] **Grievance officer**: name, designation, email and phone. The law
      requires one named person who answers complaints within one month.
- [ ] Working days and hours for phone and WhatsApp support.
- [ ] Book delivery charge (flat per order, by weight, or free above an
      amount).
- [ ] Days and hours books can be collected from the centre.
- [ ] Do you accept returns of undamaged books someone no longer wants? If
      so, within how many days, and who pays the postage?
- [ ] Your courier or postal service.
