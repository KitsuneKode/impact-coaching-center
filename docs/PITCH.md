# Pitch notes: Impact Career Tutorials

For the meeting with Raheef Sir. Everything here is checkable, and most of it
can be checked on his own phone while you sit there. Nothing in this document
is a guess.

---

## Do not open by attacking the website

He already knows it is broken. Leading with it makes him defensive and makes
you the person who came to criticise.

Open with what he has built, because it is genuinely impressive and almost
nobody has told him so:

> "Before anything else. Your podcast episode on the Ahom Moidams has thirteen
> thousand views. The one on Assamese identity has ninety-six thousand. The SP
> of Lakhimpur and the Deputy Commissioner have both sat in your classroom and
> talked to your students. You have twenty students on camera saying your name.
> Most institutes in Assam have none of that."

Then the turn:

> "Now open Google on your phone and search for your own institute."

Let him do it. He will find the 404 himself. That is worth more than any slide.

---

## The three facts

**1. His homepage does not exist.** `impactcareertutorials.in` returns 404.
So do About Us and Contact. Only `/courses` and `/freetest` still load, and the
security certificate expired in **August 2025**, so a parent opening the link on
a phone gets a red browser warning before they see anything.

**2. Google cannot read his courses page.** It is a Classplus storefront that
renders in the browser, so the page arrives empty, with no title and no text.
That means his fees, his batches and his course names are invisible in search.
When a parent in Lakhimpur searches "APSC coaching fees", he does not appear.

**3. He has no Google Business Profile.** For a centre people physically walk
into, the map result is usually the single biggest source of enquiries, ahead
of the website. He is not in it.

One more, worth raising gently: Classplus's own data flags his Android app as
delisted, while the Play listing still loads. Something is off there and he
should check it.

---

## What the new site does that the old one cannot

Show it on a phone, not a laptop. That is how his students will see it.

- **It loads, and it is fast.** 1.4 seconds to first content on a throttled
  connection.
- **His real prices are on it,** pulled from his own Classplus catalogue:
  Lakhya 2027 at ₹25,009, VICTOR 8.0 at ₹6,009, Radiant 2.0 at ₹7,009. They
  cannot drift from his checkout, because they come from it.
- **A page per course**, each with the timing off his own poster, his own
  feature list, and price data that search engines can read, so the fee can
  appear next to the Google result.
- **Every video plays on the page.** Nobody gets sent to YouTube and lost.
- **Enquiries are captured** before the WhatsApp handoff. Today, an enquiry
  that does not complete is simply gone.
- **His proof is finally visible in one place:** the two officers on the APSC
  list, his student reviews, the officers who have taught here, the podcast.

The line to use about the results page:

> "Every coaching centre in Assam puts a wall of faces on their site and lets
> you assume they made all of them. Yours says which ones you can check in the
> commission's own PDF, which ones you have announced yourself, and which ones
> were guests. That is why a parent will believe the first group."

> "You do not need this to double your admissions. You need it to produce one
> or two extra enrolments a year, and it has paid for itself. Everything above

He is also already paying Classplus for a website that returns 404.

## Objections

**"I already have a website."** It returns 404. Open it together. The Classplus
site is a good app and checkout, it is not a website that Google can read or a
parent can trust.

**"How much?"** Quote build and launch separately from ongoing. Ongoing is
small and real: prices change, batches change, results come out.

**"Who will update it?"** Nobody has to learn anything. Courses and prices come
from Classplus, which he already maintains. When he changes a price there, one
command updates the site. Enquiries land in a Google Sheet he can read on his
phone.

**"Can you also fix my Google?"** Yes, and it is the highest value thing after
launch. The Business Profile is free and takes a day.
